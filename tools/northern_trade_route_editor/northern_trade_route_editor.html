Layout editor
Northern Trade Route

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

The Northern Trade Route (rooms with an orange outline) runs from the gate north of Darkhaven, through the Shadow Grove, and winds through the Forgotten Woods. It starts off to the east of everything, with trails to the rooms it connects to, so you can drag it into place. Select the Northern Trade Route below grabs all of it at once, and Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest sends the selected rooms to where your map's own exits put them (on top of the Grove and the Woods).

The fixed rooms (small round dots, grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, the Woods' trails) is faintly behind.

The road's rooms start in the shape TinTin's own exits give them (north is up). Rooms on a level above or below ground have a dashed outline. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines.

Edit

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
