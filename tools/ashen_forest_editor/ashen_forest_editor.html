Layout editor
Ashen Forest + Eastern Trade Route

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

The Eastern Trade Route (rooms with a purple outline) is the second area. It starts just east of the forest, with a trail from each of its rooms to the forest room it connects to, so you can drag it into place. Its entrance room also links to the Darkhaven road east of the town: the row of fixed DH rooms ("The Cross Roads on the Hilltop" and the "Rock Strewn Trail" rooms). Select the Eastern Trade Route below grabs all of it at once.

The fixed rooms (diamonds with an orange outline, labelled DH, Grove or FW) already sit on the finished map in Darkhaven, the Shadow Grove and the Forgotten Woods, together with the spacer rooms between them and the forest. They can't be moved. Every forest room that connects to one has a trail running to it, so line the forest up against them. The finished map (land, lake, the Woods' trails) is faintly behind, so you can see the borders.

Rooms start exactly where you left them in your last save. Rooms on a level above ground have a dashed outline and start a little offset from the ground rooms they sit over. The Moat's 3 rooms only join the King's Castle (not on the map yet), so they start as a small separate group by the slime rivulet.

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
