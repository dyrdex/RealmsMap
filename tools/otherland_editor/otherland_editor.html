Layout editor
Otherland

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

Otherland: all its rooms, the unnamed rooms that lead only back into it, and the spacer rooms that join them. It joins the map only at the Iceberg rooms 22534 and 22535 (east to 13379 and 13380 on the Sea of Sorrows coast); its other exits (Blood Sea, Azure Sea, Sea of Sorrows, Wild Tundra, Cursed Lands, Asgard Nexus) are left off. Where a room has an up/down exit to the same room as one of its flat exits (n, e, s, w, ne, se, nw, sw), the flat exit is used and the up/down one ignored; the up/down exits left (dashed) start offset diagonally (up = north-east). The area is split into sections (parts with only a few exits between them). Each section starts where TinTin's exits put its rooms, two rooms apart; the sections are spread out with wide gaps between them over the sea west and south of the link rooms (past the map's south edge, which grows to fit), so some exits between sections (and to the map) are stretched. Hide any room you don't want on the map. Select a section grabs a whole section to move it; Select the whole piece grabs the sections of the rooms you have selected; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest moves the selected rooms back to their starting spots.

The fixed rooms (small round dots: grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods, olive for the Forest of Tears, blue for the Lake of Tich'Pyga, gold for the Western Trade Route, brown for the Ashen Forest, copper for the Southern Mountain Range and its trade route, tan for the Wastelands) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, trails) is faintly behind.

North is up. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines.

Edit

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
