Layout editor
The Azure Sea (rooms that join the map)

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

Only the Azure Sea rooms that link to rooms already on the map, and the rooms that join those to each other, are here. Each room that links to the map starts one room from the room it links to; the rooms between them are spread evenly along the shore between. Orange outline: the cliff-foot rooms under the Barren Peaks (6 have an up exit to a Peaks room, 7 lie between them). Yellow outline: the shore walk, the 'Forgotten Woods Coastline' rooms, which join the Peaks cliffs to the Azure beach room already on the map by the Ashen Forest (14254 'The Crest of the Hill'); none of them has an exit into a Forgotten Woods room. Green outline: the coast north from the cliffs (14284, 14285, 14287 'Azure and Green' to 14313 and a spacer room) up to 4317 'Rocky Coastline', which joins the north of the Plains (4316) through two spacer rooms (14314, 22557: already on the map, movable here, starting where they are). Pink outline: the path from 4317 north to the Village of Edo (7746 'Adrift off the Coast' leads nw into Edo); it starts north of 4317 as TinTin's exits lay it out. Hide any room you don't want on the map. Select the whole piece grabs every room in the same piece as the rooms you have selected; the three Select buttons grab each group; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest moves the selected rooms to where TinTin's exits alone would put them.

The fixed rooms (small round dots, grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods, brown for the Northern Trade Route, rust for the Barren Peaks, wheat for the Northern Plains, grey for the Ashen Forest and its beach) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, the Woods' trails) is faintly behind.

North is up. Rooms that TinTin puts on a level above or below the entrance have a dashed outline. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines; these rooms are all on one level (their links to the Peaks are up exits).

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
