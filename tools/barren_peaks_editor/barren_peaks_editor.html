Layout editor
Barren Peaks of Tahjliera

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

The Barren Peaks (rooms with an orange outline) and the Northern Trade Route past the long bridge (rooms with a gold outline, the road the Peaks are entered from) start off to the north of everything, in the shapes TinTin's exits give them, with trails to the Forgotten Woods rooms they connect to, so you can drag them into place. They come in a few separate pieces (one large, the rest small). Select the whole piece grabs every room in the same piece as the rooms you have selected; Select the Barren Peaks grabs everything movable; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest sends the selected rooms to where your map's own exits put them (against the north edge of the Woods).

The Forgotten Woods (rooms with a green outline) are unlocked (Oct 19): move them, hide them or show them like the Peaks. They start exactly where the finished map has them.

The fixed rooms (small round dots, grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods, brown for the Northern Trade Route up to the bridge) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, the Woods' trails) is faintly behind.

North is up. Rooms that TinTin puts on a level above or below the entrance have a dashed outline. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines; most of the Peaks' up/down rooms are also joined sideways through spacer rooms, so they start beside the ground level rather than on top of it.

Edit

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
