Layout editor
The Southern Mountain Range

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

Every room of the Southern Mountain Range (copper outline) and of the Southern Mountain Trade Route (teal outline; it starts where TinTin's exits lay it out from the range), plus the spacer rooms that join them to each other or to the map, and the Forest of Tears rooms along the whole Sparse Woodlands trail (rooms 1514-1553 whatever their names, and the spacer rooms between them; green outline, unlocked: they start where the map has them). Nothing is placed by hand yet: each piece of the range starts where TinTin's own exits put it, anchored (shifted, and stretched a little per axis when it touches the map in several places) to the Forest of Tears rooms it links to. Expect it to overlap itself and the forest until you move it. Rooms with no exit to anything here are parked in a column east of the range. Hide any room you don't want on the map. Select the whole piece grabs every room in the same piece as the rooms you have selected; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest moves the selected rooms to where TinTin's exits alone would put them.

The fixed rooms (small round dots, apart from the unlocked Sparse Woodlands rooms: grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods, olive for the Forest of Tears, blue for the Lake of Tich'Pyga, gold for the Western Trade Route) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, trails) is faintly behind.

North is up. Rooms that TinTin puts on a level above or below ground (the tunnels, caverns and underground rivers) have a dashed outline and start a little to the side of the ground room they sit under or over. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines.

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
