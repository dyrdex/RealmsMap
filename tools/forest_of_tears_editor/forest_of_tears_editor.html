Layout editor
The Forest of Tears and the Lake of Tich'Pyga

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

Every room of the Forest of Tears (green outline) and of the Lake of Tich'Pyga (blue outline), and the Western Trade Route's drawn road (gold outline, unlocked: it starts where the map has it), and Forgotten Woods rooms 5634-5638 (pink outline, unlocked: they start where the map has them), is here, plus the spacer rooms that join them to each other or to the map. The forest starts exactly where the finished map has it. The lake has no rooms on the map today (it is one drawn lake), so it starts as TinTin's own layout of it, stretched so that the rooms that touch the forest, the Forgotten Woods and Darkhaven land by the rooms they link to; expect it to sit on top of some forest rooms until you move it. A column of rooms with no exit to anything here (lake rooms with no exits, and Forest of Tears room 357, whose only exit is to the Southern Mountain Range) is parked just east of the lake. Hide any room you don't want on the map. Select the whole piece grabs every room in the same piece as the rooms you have selected; the Select buttons grab each area; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest moves the selected rooms to where TinTin's exits alone would put them.

The fixed rooms (small round dots, grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, trails) is faintly behind; the forest and lake are going to be redrawn from your layout.

North is up. Rooms that TinTin puts on a level above or below the entrance (the Treetops, the cliffs, the lake bed) have a dashed outline and start a little to the side of the ground room they sit over. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines.

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
