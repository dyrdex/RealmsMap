Layout editor
Wastelands

Arrange the rooms exactly how you want them. Paths are drawn from the real exits between rooms, so they follow wherever you put the rooms.

  • Drag a room to move it. Its paths follow.
  • Shift-drag empty space (or use Box select) to grab many rooms. Shift-click adds or removes one.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift = bigger, Alt = finer). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • Scroll to zoom, drag empty space to pan.

The Wastelands: all its 351 rooms (the 13 underwater rooms and the 6 tunnel rooms included) and the spacer rooms that join them. Its exits into the Blood Sea and the Sea of Sorrows are left off, and so are the other areas it leads to. The rooms start where TinTin's exits put them, one room apart, from the Southern Mountain Range rooms 34-36 and the Southern Mountain Trade Route rooms 9339 and 9346; rooms on another level are offset diagonally (up = north-east). Rooms with no exit to the rest are parked just east of the area. The map grows south to fit them. Hide any room you don't want on the map. Select the whole piece grabs every room in the same piece as the rooms you have selected; Selected → where TinTin's exits suggest moves the selected rooms back to their starting spots.

The fixed rooms (small round dots: grey-blue for Darkhaven, violet for the Shadow Grove, green for the Forgotten Woods, olive for the Forest of Tears, blue for the Lake of Tich'Pyga, gold for the Western Trade Route, brown for the Ashen Forest, copper for the Southern Mountain Range and its trade route) are the rooms already on the finished map, exactly where they are. They can't be moved. Hover one to see its name. The faint lines between them are the exits between those rooms. The finished map (land, lake, trails) is faintly behind.

North is up. Up and down exits are dashed purple lines.

Edit

On the final map

Flag the rooms whose paths you don't want drawn on the map. A trail is drawn only when both of its rooms are shown. Hidden rooms fade out with a red ✕. (H hides the selection, Shift+H shows it.)
