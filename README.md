# Realm Map

The live map is built and published to GitHub Pages automatically on every push (see `.github/workflows/pages.yml`). `realm_map.html` is the full map as one self-contained page; it is also the source the tiles are rendered from.

## Rebuilding from source
The `src` folder holds everything used to make the map. To rebuild, run `python3 build.py` inside `src` (Python 3 with numpy, scipy, scikit-image, Pillow and matplotlib). It reruns the whole pipeline from your TinTin++ map file and rewrites `realm_map.html` next to this file. The painting uses fixed random seeds, so every rebuild comes out the same (the v9 bundle was rebuilt from these files and matched the published map exactly).

- `world` is your TinTin++ map file; `parse_world.py` reads it.
- `extract.py` and `extract2.py` place New Darkhaven's rooms, streets, gates and buildings (writes `nd2.json`).
- `fot_extract.py` paints the Forest of Tears, the Western Trade Route, Miden'nir, the Shadow Grove and the Forgotten Woods, traces the routes to places outside the town, and tracks every Darkhaven, Grove and Woods room to where it ends up on the finished map (writes `fot.json`).
- `data.py` holds your list of areas and the directions you gave. `fw_final.json` is the Forgotten Woods layout you arranged.
- `scale_regions.py`, `apply_directions.py`, `lake_reshape.py`, `gap_fill.py`, `arrange_apply.py`, `partition.py`, `label_tweaks.py`, `arrangement_1.json`: as in earlier versions (scaling, the town cards' directions, Lake Tich, the arrangement of pieces, the big area names). `build_arranger.py` and `arranger.js` make the arranger page from a finished map.

### The Ashen Forest and the Eastern Trade Route (version 8)
`ashen_layout_final.json` (the layout you saved from the Ashen Forest editor), `ashen_editor_edges.json`, `ashen_removed_rooms.json`, `ashen_cut_links.json`, `ashen_forest.py` (puts that layout on the map), `visual_nudges.py` (the few rooms drawn slightly off their layout position: the ring of road round the Ashen Forest Inn), `af_marker_routes.py` + `af_marker_routes.json` and `af_markers.py` (the markers off the forest and road), `dh_paths.py` (the road east from the Darkpass gate and the path southeast from the Cross Roads), `east_shore.py` (the beach and ocean).

### New in version 9: the Northern Trade Route, its sub-areas and the coast
- `ntr_layout_final.json` is the layout you saved from the Northern Trade Route editor (the 54 road rooms placed among the 1,089 fixed rooms of Darkhaven, the Shadow Grove and the Forgotten Woods). `ntr_editor_edges.json` is the list of trails that touch the road.
- `ntr_merge.py` puts the road on the map as one continuous formal road (the same style as the Eastern Trade Route), with an off-ramp pad and a short dirt connector at each place it joins the Woods or the Grove, and moves the Maze of Trees' name clear of it.
- `ntr_marker_routes.py` + `ntr_marker_routes.json` follow your directions to the City of Salburg, the Keep of Belial and the Wolf's Den; `ntr_markers.py` places them, with their links (each leaves the road at an off-ramp pad), the forest that fills the indent east of the Maze of Trees, and the straight coast down past Salburg.
- `bay.py` is the one rounded bowl that is Ocean Keep's small bay (used by `ntr_markers.py` and `scenery.py`). `west_coast.py` draws the new smooth west coast from the Forgotten Woods down to the middle of the Forest of Tears.
- `scenery.py` holds the later touches: the castle, windmill, town, keep and inn drawings, the Ashen Forest name, Ocean Keep's bay shore and its shallow water, the darker ground at Shai'ghool and Shadar Logoth, and buildings taken off the map.
- `template.html` is the map page; `build.py` fills it with the data. `VERSION` is the label in the footer.

### The tiled map (after version 9)
The map is dark mode only: one dark palette, no light theme and no theme switch. `build.py` now ends by running `site_build.py`, which bakes the terrain into image tiles and writes the fast tiled page to `site/` (open it through a web server, e.g. `python3 -m http.server` inside `site`) and the same page as one file, `realm_map_preview.html`, which opens anywhere. `site_template.html` is that page (sidebar, search, cards, routes, legend over a Leaflet map); `vendor/` holds Leaflet 1.9.4 and `fonts/` the Alegreya fonts used for the bake. This step needs Playwright with Chromium (`pip install playwright`, `playwright install chromium`); set `NO_TILES=1` to skip it. `docs/tiles_poc/measure.py` compares the old page with the tiled one.

To re-merge after changing a layout in an editor: replace `ashen_layout_final.json` or `ntr_layout_final.json` with the new save (and, if rooms or exits changed, rebuild the matching `*_edges.json` with the editor's build script), then run `python3 build.py`.

To continue in a new conversation, open this repository in Claude Code; `CLAUDE.md` holds the working notes and `docs/REGION_NOTES.md` the detailed history of each region. Install the packages with `pip install -r requirements.txt`. The tiled site, the preview page, `nd2.json` and `fot.json` are build outputs and are not kept in the repository.

## Tools
- `tools/forgotten_woods_editor.html` is the drag-to-arrange layout editor for the Forgotten Woods, with `forgotten_woods_layout_final_save.json`, the layout you saved from it, and `map_arrangement_1_as_saved.json`, the arrangement as the arranger saved it.
- `tools/ashen_forest_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Ashen Forest and the Eastern Trade Route (`ashen_forest_editor.html`, the layout you saved, and `build_ashen_editor.py` with its template). It needs a dump of the map just before the forest is added: run `ASHEN_DUMP=/tmp/w_before_ashen.json python3 build.py`, then `python3 build_ashen_editor.py /tmp/w_before_ashen.json ashen_forest_editor.html`.
- `tools/northern_trade_route_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Northern Trade Route: `northern_trade_route_editor.html` (opens in any browser; **Save for Claude** works only on the published page, but Download file and Copy / paste text work anywhere), the layout you saved, and `build_ntr_editor.py` with `ntr_editor_template.html` (and `make_ntr_template.py`, which makes that template from the Ashen Forest editor's). To rebuild it, copy `build_ntr_editor.py` and `ntr_editor_template.html` into `src`, run `FINAL_DUMP=/tmp/w_final.json python3 build.py`, then `python3 build_ntr_editor.py /tmp/w_final.json northern_trade_route_editor.html`. It shows every room already on the finished map as a fixed dot and the road's rooms as movable ones.
- `tools/barren_peaks_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Barren Peaks (`barren_peaks_editor.html`, `build_bp_editor.py`, `bp_editor_template.html` and `make_bp_template.py`, which makes that template from the Northern Trade Route editor's). It shows every room already on the map, including the Northern Trade Route up to the bridge, as fixed dots, and the Barren Peaks, the road past the bridge and their spacer rooms as movable ones, in pieces. To rebuild it, copy `build_bp_editor.py` and `bp_editor_template.html` into `src`, run `FINAL_DUMP=/tmp/w_final.json NO_TILES=1 python3 build.py`, then `python3 build_bp_editor.py /tmp/w_final.json barren_peaks_editor.html`.
- `src/bp_merge.py` puts the Barren Peaks on the map from `src/bp_layout_final.json` (the layout saved in the editor) and `src/bp_editor_edges.json` (the editor's rooms and exits): the road past the bridge, the Peaks' trails, new land with mountains. It runs after every other change to the land and uses its own taller raster grid.
- `src/bp_markers.py` adds the markers for the areas that open off the Barren Peaks, using `src/bp_marker_routes.json`, which the one-time script `src/bp_marker_routes.py` writes by following your directions through your TinTin map.
- `tools/northern_plains_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Northern Plains (`northern_plains_editor.html`, `build_np_editor.py`, `np_editor_template.html` and `make_np_template.py`, which makes that template from the Barren Peaks editor's). Every room already on the map, including the Barren Peaks as you arranged them, is a fixed dot; the Plains and the Northern Trade Route past the long bridge are movable. To rebuild it, copy `build_np_editor.py` and `np_editor_template.html` into `src`, run `FINAL_DUMP=/tmp/w_final.json NO_TILES=1 python3 build.py`, then `python3 build_np_editor.py /tmp/w_final.json northern_plains_editor.html` (it also writes `src/np_editor_edges.json`, the editor's rooms and exits).
- `src/np_merge.py` puts the Northern Plains on the map from `src/np_layout_final.json` and `src/np_editor_edges.json`: wheat-coloured ground with tufts and brush, and paths only for the links to the Barren Peaks and the paths listed in it (the Northern Trade Route itself is drawn by the Barren Peaks merge, with its Plains rooms where the Plains layout put them). It runs after every other change to the land.
- `src/np_markers.py` adds the markers for the areas that open off the Northern Plains, using `src/np_marker_routes.json`, which the one-time script `src/np_marker_routes.py` writes by following your directions through your TinTin map.
- `tools/azure_sea_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Azure Sea rooms that join the map (`azure_sea_editor.html`, `build_az_editor.py`, `az_editor_template.html` and `make_az_template.py`, which makes that template from the Northern Plains editor's): the 49 Azure Sea rooms below the Barren Peaks cliffs, along the shore to the Ashen beach and up the coast to the north of the Plains and on to the Village of Edo are movable; every room on the map is a fixed dot. To rebuild it, copy `build_az_editor.py` and `az_editor_template.html` into `src`, run `FINAL_DUMP=/tmp/w_final.json NO_TILES=1 python3 build.py`, then `python3 build_az_editor.py /tmp/w_final.json azure_sea_editor.html` (it also writes `src/az_editor_edges.json`).
- `src/az_merge.py` puts the Azure Sea Coast on the map from `src/az_layout_final.json` and `src/az_editor_edges.json`: a dark blue water route along the foot of the cliffs, a small stream at its south end (14257 to 14265), open sea east of the mainland out past the route, a cliff edge along that coast, small dashed lines for the hidden rooms' links, and the Village of Edo marker at 7746. It is the last change to the land.
- `tools/forest_of_tears_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Forest of Tears, the Lake of Tich'Pyga and the Western Trade Route (`forest_of_tears_editor.html`, `build_fot_editor.py`, `fot_editor_template.html` and `make_fot_template.py`, which makes that template from the Azure Sea editor's). `src/fot_extract.py` now also tracks the forest's rooms and the road's rooms (`ft<id>`, `wt<id>`); `build.py` removes those keys before writing `realm_map.html`.
- `tools/southern_mountain_range_editor/` holds the layout editor for the Southern Mountain Range (`southern_mountain_range_editor.html`, `build_smr_editor.py`, `smr_editor_template.html` and `make_smr_template.py`, which makes that template from the Forest of Tears editor's). Rebuild like the others; edges in `src/smr_editor_edges.json`.
- `src/smr_merge.py` draws the Southern Mountain Range, its Trade Route and the moved Sparse Woodlands trail from `src/smr_layout_final.json` + `src/smr_editor_edges.json` (see docs/REGION_NOTES.md); `build.py` passes the moved trail's positions to `ft_merge` first.
- `tools/midennir_editor/` holds the Miden'nir layout editor (`midennir_editor.html`, `build_md_editor.py`, `md_editor_template.html` made by `make_md_template.py`): Miden'nir's above-ground rooms are movable, every room already on the map is fixed. `src/md_editor_edges.json` is written with it. `src/md_merge.py` draws the rooms you kept from your save (`src/md_layout_final.json`) on the painted Miden'nir.
- `tools/pixie_forest_editor/` holds the Pixie Forest layout editor (`pixie_forest_editor.html`, `build_pf_editor.py`, `pf_editor_template.html` made by `make_pf_template.py`): the Pixie Forest and Asgard Nexus rooms you listed, and Forgotten Woods rooms 4017-4019, are movable. `src/pf_editor_edges.json` is written with it. `src/pf_merge.py` draws your save (`src/pf_layout_final.json`): the faint Pixie Forest trail and the moved Woods rooms.
- `tools/coral_depths_editor/` holds the Coral Depths layout editor (`coral_depths_editor.html`, `build_cd_editor.py`, `cd_editor_template.html` made by `make_cd_template.py` from the Pixie Forest template): the 47 Azure Sea / Coral Depths rooms you listed (plus their joining spacers) and map rooms 7-10, 569-572, 6365, 19863 (Southern Mountain Range) and 14360 (Ashen Forest) are movable. `src/cd_editor_edges.json` is written with it.
- `src/extra_places.py`: places added by hand (Suds 'n Stones, A'enari) and V'laresch's move to the Mobs list.
- `src/route_options.py`: places with a default route and alternate routes (your `ROUTE_OPTIONS` list in `src/data.py`: direction word, room count, note, directions). Each route is traced to the place's marker; the pop-up lists the alternates, and ticking one draws it.
- `src/owner_edits.py`: small hand edits applied last (lines taken out, places taken out); `src/smr_clearings.json`: hand-made clearings, towns (Edel), climbs and rooms added to clearings for the Southern Mountain Range.
- `src/ft_merge.py` puts the Forest of Tears, the lake and the Western Trade Route on the map from `src/ft_layout_final.json` and `src/fot_editor_edges.json` (see `docs/REGION_NOTES.md`). It runs after the Azure Sea Coast merge.
